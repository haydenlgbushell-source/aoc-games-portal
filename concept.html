Closer look at key ideas

New feature

Team Australia athlete spotlight

Each HP Manager would see the athletes they manage as profile cards on the Home tab — name, sport, qualification status, and a direct link to their documents and medical forms. Currently this information is scattered across sections.

1Athlete name, sport, and competition schedule at a glance
2Qualification status linked to live entry standards
3One-click access to athlete's medical, travel, and competition documents
4Flag indicator for outstanding forms or missing declarations

Concept

Interactive Dakar venue map

An SVG map of Dakar showing competition venues, the Olympic Village, the polyclinic, and transport routes. Hovering a venue shows its schedule, AOC room allocation, and relevant documents. Currently there's no visual representation of the city.

1Click any venue to see the competition schedule and AOC allocation
2Transport route overlay showing bus loops between venues
3Distance and travel time from the Olympic Village
4Direct link to venue-specific documents from the map pin

Enhancement

Richer data visualisation

Replace the current text-based KPI strip with interactive charts showing preparation progress, quota completions, and deadline status at a glance. HP Managers and Data roles can see their key metrics without opening a spreadsheet.

1Donut chart showing quota completion by sport
2Timeline view of deadlines across the preparation period
3Document completion rate per section with drill-down
4Real-time sync from SharePoint lists (no manual updates)
